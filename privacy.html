Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This policy describes the current privacy practices of VYBORA, a locally operated affiliate campaign preparation application. It must be updated before any new online account connection, analytics, or publishing integration is enabled.

Current application status: VYBORA currently runs on the operator's Windows computer. Campaign records and generated content are stored locally in a SQLite database on that computer. This public website is an information and policy page; it is not the VYBORA application.

1. Information handled by the local application

The local application may store affiliate URLs, product names, product categories, selected target markets, product-page descriptions, campaign drafts, creative settings and timestamps. These records are stored on the operator's computer in the application's local database.

2. Product-page retrieval

When the operator requests product-page analysis, the application may request publicly accessible page information from the URL entered by the operator. The availability of that information depends on the merchant website. The application should not be used to bypass login controls or access restrictions.

3. Pinterest and other social accounts

The current version described by this policy does not automatically publish content to Pinterest and does not currently connect to a Pinterest account. If an official API integration is added, this policy will be updated before activation to explain the data accessed, permissions requested, purpose of use, storage and deletion practices. Account authorization will require the account owner's consent.

4. Sharing and sale of information

The current local application is not designed to sell campaign data or share its local database with advertisers. If future integrations transmit information to social platforms or other service providers, those transfers and their purposes will be described in an updated policy.

5. Data retention and deletion

Campaign data remains in the local database until the operator deletes it or removes the application data. The operator should keep backups secure and avoid sharing the database because it may contain affiliate tracking URLs.

6. Security

The operator is responsible for securing the Windows computer and protecting local files. Do not place passwords, API secrets or access tokens in public repositories, campaign descriptions or screenshots. Any future credentials must be stored separately from this public website.

7. Third-party websites

Affiliate and merchant links may lead to third-party websites. Those websites have their own privacy policies and terms. VYBORA does not control their privacy practices.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy will be revised if VYBORA's data handling or integrations change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.
