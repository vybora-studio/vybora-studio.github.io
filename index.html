VYBORA
Affiliate Campaign Studio

Prepare better affiliate campaigns.

VYBORA is a locally operated tool in development that helps its owner organize affiliate campaigns, prepare marketing copy and create promotional graphics.

What VYBORA does

  • Stores affiliate campaign details on the owner's Windows computer.
  • Extracts publicly available product-page information where the page permits access.
  • Prepares draft promotional content for social platforms.
  • Creates downloadable promotional graphics using local browser-based templates.
  • Prepares Pinterest Pin titles, descriptions, disclosures and destination URLs.

Current status

VYBORA is a local application under development. Its current version prepares campaign content and creative assets. It does not currently publish Pins automatically or connect to Pinterest accounts. Any future Pinterest integration will require the account owner's explicit authorization and the permissions approved by Pinterest.

Affiliate disclosure

VYBORA is intended to help prepare affiliate promotional materials. The operator may earn a commission when someone purchases through an affiliate link, at no additional cost to the buyer. Affiliate disclosures are included in promotional drafts where configured.
